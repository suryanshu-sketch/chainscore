What's actually different

Two things no other tool does.

McAfee and Avast already combine signals into a score — but they're proprietary, paid, and don't publish their weights. ChainScore is free, transparent, and goes further.

BLAST-RADIUS AWARE

Not every reused password is equally dangerous

Reusing a password between two throwaway accounts is minor. Reusing it between a throwaway account and your recovery email is not — that breach can cascade into everything the email can reset. ChainScore weighs reuse by what's actually at stake.

Frequently asked

Do you store my passwords?

No. Passwords are analyzed for strength and reuse in your session only — never stored in plaintext, never sent anywhere in full.

How is breach exposure checked?

Via the k-anonymity method — only a short hash prefix is ever transmitted, never your actual password.

Is the scoring formula public?

Yes — every weight is rule-based and documented, unlike proprietary vendor scores.

Is this free?

Yes. ChainScore is a free, open scoring project — no subscription required.
